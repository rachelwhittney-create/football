Where do I find espn_s2 and SWID?
  1. On a computer, sign in at fantasy.espn.com and open your league.
  2. Open the browser's developer tools (Chrome: right-click → Inspect, or F12).
  3. Go to Application (Safari/Firefox: Storage) → Cookies → https://fantasy.espn.com.
  4. Copy the value of espn_s2 and of SWID (keep the curly braces) into the boxes above.

These work like a key to your ESPN account. They're kept only in this browser and sent to ESPN to read your league. They are never saved on the server. They expire now and then; just copy fresh ones. Public leagues only need the League ID.
